Global headlines

Developing. Iranian claim, only partly confirmed. AP report

Iran says tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz

Iranian state TV said three tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines overnight into Monday; the Revolutionary Guard navy says six tankers have been damaged since Sunday and warned that the US-protected route is still dangerous. UKMTO reported only one tanker hit by an “unknown projectile”, with all crew accounted for. The strait normally carries about a fifth of the world's oil and LNG.

Claims disputed. Al Jazeera with Reuters and AP

Megaspeed says Alice Huang left in 2025; Singapore bars her

The Nvidia GPU buyer at the centre of the Jensen Huang scandal says she quit as an executive in August 2025 and has hired a US law firm to audit export compliance. Singapore has barred her from re-entering, saying she misled investigators; Beijing calls the espionage allegations “entirely groundless”.

No deal yet. Reported by Al Jazeera

ASEAN and China miss South China Sea code deal

Talks in Manila reportedly agreed about 80% of a draft but not whether it would be binding: “There is no conclusion yet,” said Philippine foreign minister Theresa Lazaro. ASEAN also extended its summit ban on Myanmar's leader.

German politics

Koalitionsausschuss today. Reported by ZDFheute

Merz: „Wir werden uns morgen einigen“ on the Stromsteuer

Speaking in Munich alongside Söder, Merz said today's coalition committee will cut the electricity tax to the EU minimum for 2027, including for households. No deal with the SPD was announced yet: Klingbeil wants the cut counter-financed, and the CSU rejects reversing the Mütterrente. Pensions, electoral law and the Existenzminimum are also on the agenda.

Read at ZDFheute
State government. Reported by Der Nordschleswiger (dpa)

Schwesig re-elected in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern

The Landtag confirmed Manuela Schwesig (SPD) with 39 votes to 29, at the head of a new SPD-Linke-Grüne coalition that holds 39 of 71 seats. The AfD is the only opposition party.

Read at Der Nordschleswiger
Party congress in January. Reported by ZDFheute

Hubertus Heil runs for SPD chair

The former labour minister is the first declared candidate to succeed Bärbel Bas; co-chair Klingbeil has not said whether he will run again. ZDF's correspondent called the bid a „Frontalangriff“ on Klingbeil.

Spanish politics

Election 29 November. Reported by Infobae

Sánchez announces 11% public-sector pay rise as Lula backs him

In a Telecinco interview, Sánchez said today's Council of Ministers will approve an 11% pay rise for public employees for 2025–2028, with 4.5% from 1 January 2027, and confirmed a 2027 minimum-wage increase. At Moncloa, Brazil's Lula called him „la persona indicada para seguir defendiendo la democracia en Europa“.

About 200 people threw stones, firecrackers and eggs at candidate Jon Echeverría's team on Saturday. The PP vasco says „no se dan las condiciones democráticas suficientes“; the PNV's Aitor Esteban called the attack „intolerable“.

Rail and public transport

No reopening date. Bahnblogstelle (dpa)

Berlin–Hannover diversion route hit by damage near Magdeburg

A mowing vehicle damaged signalling equipment near Niederndodeleben, DB says. The Magdeburg line is the detour while Berlin–Stendal–Wolfsburg–Hannover is rebuilt, so some ICs and ICEs now run via Hamburg or are cancelled. The Verband der Güterbahnen called it a „Drama mit Ansage“.

First look at Frankfurt's long-distance underground station

DB InfraGO showed a design with a „wie gefaltet“ ceiling and room for four trains at once. The site is to be fixed by May 2027; construction starts in the 2030s, and the 2018 cost estimate of €3.6bn needs updating.

Topic of the day: Self-hosting

New project. Found via selfh.st, checked on GitHub

plMail: a self-hosted, Gmail-style client for all your accounts

It syncs IMAP, Gmail and Outlook accounts into your own PostgreSQL database, with push mail, full-text search, filters and a calendar with Google, Microsoft and CalDAV sync. It is not a mail server. AGPL-3.0, built in PHP (Symfony), deployable with Docker Compose or from the TrueNAS catalogue; optional AI features via Ollama are off by default.
