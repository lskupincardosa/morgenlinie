German politics

Cabinet draft, not yet law. ZDFheute

Cabinet loosens Kündigungsschutz for top earners and extends fixed-term contracts

Employers could end contracts of staff earning about €186,000 a year or more against court-set severance, for 12 months after the law takes effect. Fixed-term contracts without a reason could run four years with up to six extensions, for hires until end-2030. Labour Minister Alexander Schweitzer (SPD) said firms must be able to act „so schnell wie möglich“; the bill is to be fast-tracked.
