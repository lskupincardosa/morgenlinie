German politics

Decided, 2027 only. cash (dpa-AFX, Handelsblatt)

Koalitionsausschuss: Stromsteuer cut and Spritpreisdeckel for 2027

The Stromsteuer falls from 2.05 to 0.1 ct/kWh for all consumers from 1 January 2027, costing about €5.4bn a year; 2027 is covered from EEG-account reserves, and continuing it needs „eine nachhaltige Gegenfinanzierung im Bundeshaushalt 2028“. Merz: „Wir haben die Senkung der Stromsteuer versprochen – und wir haben Wort gehalten.“ A fuel-price cap is to replace the Tankrabatt from January for three months; its design is open, with a cabinet decision due on 2 December.

Rail and public transport

Needs Bundestag approval. VerkehrsRundschau, Bahnblogstelle

Bund presents tougher performance contract for DB InfraGO

Minister Steffen Bilger (CDU) presented a five-year Leistungsvereinbarung from 2027 with 40 performance indicators and sanctions, modelled on Switzerland; „Der Bund stellt in den kommenden fünf Jahren mehr als 80 Milliarden Euro“. The target is 70% long-distance punctuality by 2029; it still needs the Haushaltsausschuss and DB supervisory board.

Global aviation

Cause unclear. Reported by aeroTELEGRAPH

FAA halts new Juniper routers after nationwide ground stop

After the 13 November ground stop, the FAA suspended installation of new Juniper routers and switched off those fitted. An overloaded router in El Dorado, Arkansas, disrupted communications and radar data at 17 of 22 regional control centres; the FAA is reverting to 25-year-old Cisco routers while L3Harris examines the devices.
