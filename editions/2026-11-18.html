Global headlines

No date for next round. Reported by Al Jazeera

Ukraine talks with US and European envoys; next step in Berlin

Zelenskyy held online talks with Witkoff, Kushner and representatives of France, Germany, Britain, Italy and Poland; they agreed to meet the Coalition of the Willing in Berlin. Zelenskyy says Europe will be represented in future talks with Russia, but Kremlin aide Ushakov said Moscow has not agreed to that. Kushner: “a final agreement will require difficult decisions.” Ukraine struck the Crimea Bridge again overnight; the extent of damage is unclear.

Read at Al Jazeera
New export rule. Reported by Al Jazeera

US moves to block China's remote access to AI chips

The Commerce Department's BIS now requires export licences for advanced chips to Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil, citing “unacceptable risk of diversion”. Commerce and Justice also confirmed a criminal investigation into Megaspeed; Nvidia shares fell 4.1% on Monday.

Read at Al Jazeera
Developing. Iranian claims unverified. Al Jazeera with Reuters

UKMTO reports two tankers attacked near Hormuz

One tanker was disabled by an engine-room fire 7 nm north of Khasab, Oman; another was hit by a projectile. No casualties. Iran claims mine strikes, but it is unclear whether these are the same incidents.

Read at Al Jazeera
Army figures unverified. Reported by Al Jazeera

Yemen: government forces regroup to defend Aden as Houthis advance

Government-aligned forces pulled back from positions near Bab al-Mandeb, and the Houthis reportedly cut a Red Sea coastal supply route. Parliament voted to designate the Houthis a “terrorist organisation”.

Read at Al Jazeera

German politics

Developing. Tagesspiegel (dpa, AFP, Reuters)

Koalitionsausschuss agrees Stromsteuer cut for 2027

After talks from 17:30 to about 2:00, CDU, CSU and SPD agreed to cut the electricity tax to the EU minimum for all consumers from 1 January 2027, initially until the end of 2027 (about €5bn a year). How it will be financed was not reported; a written statement was still to come. No outcomes were reported yet on pensions, Wahlrecht or the fuel-price cap.

Read at Tagesspiegel
Planned for 2027. Reported by ZDFheute

Germany and four EU states seek “return hub” in Rwanda

Germany, Austria, Denmark, the Netherlands and Greece want to send rejected asylum seekers there. Dobrindt (CSU) says it will respect human rights; development minister Alabali Radovan (SPD) has reservations, and Caritas and the IRC criticise the plan.

Read at ZDFheute

Spanish politics

Approved. Reported by elDiario.es

Council of Ministers approves 4.5% pay rise for public employees

The decree covers more than three million workers from January 2027, under the 2025 deal with CCOO, UGT and CSIF; minister Óscar López called it the largest rise since 1992 and „un orgullo“. The Diputación Permanente must still validate it. Infobae reports the government also approved a minimum wage of €1,286 a month.

Read at elDiario.esInfobae live
Polls differ. Reported by Infobae

Most polls give PP and Vox a majority

NC Report, Sigma Dos, GAD3, DYM and InvyMark project an absolute majority for PP and Vox; the CIS and SW Demoscopia put the PSOE ahead. Eleven days before 29-N.

Read at Infobae
Preliminary findings. Reported by Público

CNI finds no evidence Morocco orchestrated the Ceuta entry

A report to Audiencia Nacional judge María Tardón found no evidence the July mass entry was „instigada, planeada o coordinada“ by Rabat, describing a social-media call and border failures.

Read at Público

Rail and public transport

Funding agreement in 2027. Bahnblogstelle (dpa)

München–Mühldorf upgrade: first financing step in sight

Federal transport minister Steffen Bilger (CDU) says the federal government will sign a financing agreement with DB in 2027 for a first section west of Dorfen (€115m) of the ABS 38, estimated at €4.5bn in total; Bavaria expects construction to start by 2029. The Greens and VCD say overall funding is not yet secured.

Read at Bahnblogstelle
Follow-up. Bahnblogstelle (dpa, EVN)

Berlin–Hannover diversion via Magdeburg running again

After Monday's signalling damage, freight trains returned on Tuesday and regular service resumes this morning. About 100 trains were diverted or cancelled.

Global aviation

Deadline 23 November. Reported by aeroTELEGRAPH

Lufthansa and Vereinigung Cockpit deadlocked

After 18 mediation rounds, Lufthansa rejects the mediators' proposal and wants its own offer as the basis. VC gives it until 23 November to return to talks: „Wer sämtliche Lösungsansätze ablehnt, macht eine Einigung unmöglich.“ No strike has been announced.

Read at aeroTELEGRAPH
Opening 2029. Reported by aeroTELEGRAPH

Ryanair to build €500m engine centre in Seville

Its first engine MRO site, for CFM56-7B and LEAP-1B engines, with more than 600 jobs; construction starts in early 2027.

Read at aeroTELEGRAPH
Cabin refit. Reported by aeroTELEGRAPH

Lufthansa A380's old business class gone by March

The A380s get Thompson Vantage XL seats, not Allegris; business class drops from 78 to 68 seats. All eight aircraft are to be converted by summer 2027.

Read at aeroTELEGRAPH

Topic of the day: Open source

Governance. Manjaro forum, via LWN

Manjaro Project e.V. founded, independent of Manjaro GmbH

After a governance dispute, Manjaro team members registered a non-profit in Berlin, separate from the commercial Manjaro GmbH & Co KG. Company assets have not been transferred yet; an 18 November update says talks with the company have begun.

Read on the Manjaro forum

Rust 1.100.0 stabilises the never type

It also stabilises the core Allocator trait, and Cargo now warns about unused dependencies and supports a minimum publish age for new crate versions.
