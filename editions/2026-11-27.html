Rail and public transport

Policy. Zughalt.de

Bund to steer DB InfraGO with penalties and KPIs

Transport Minister Steffen Bilger (CDU) wants a new Leistungsvereinbarung to replace the LuFV III from 1 January 2027, on a Swiss-style model. The draft puts about €83 billion in federal funds through 2031 and covers 13 corridor renovations, down from 21, plus 500 stations. Penalties could reach €1 million a week for late reports. Approval by parliamentary bodies and DB's own bodies is still pending.

Topic of the day: Best of the week

Pick of the week. CachyOS blog

CachyOS adds a built-in recovery system and EAC v2 support

The November release adds cachyos-recovery, a recovery environment of about 70 MB on the ESP. It boots from the bootloader or UEFI, can boot and roll back Btrfs snapshots, and regenerates kernels and boot configs without a live USB. A signed linux-cachyos-eac kernel also enables Easy Anti-Cheat v2, which needs Secure Boot and TPM 2.0. After updating, run sudo chwd -a -f.

Read the release notes
Licensing. LLVM Discourse

LLVM closes its eight-year relicensing effort

Over 99.5% of contributed code is now Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception or otherwise covered. File-level updates ship with LLVM 25.
