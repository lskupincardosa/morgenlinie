Global headlines

Under investigation. Al Jazeera

Poland reports three Russian airspace violations overnight

Defence Minister Władysław Kosiniak-Kamysz said the objects were probably two missiles and a drone; sites near Nowy Lipowiec, Mołodycz and Rudnik nad Sanem are being examined, and an explosion was reported in Biłgoraj County. Donald Tusk called it a „provocation“ during exercises with the Coalition of the Willing. NATO's Mark Rutte called it „clearly dangerous“ and visits Warsaw today. No casualties were reported.

Overtime, no deal yet. Al Jazeera

COP31 in Antalya runs over as fossil-fuel language is dropped

A slimmer draft removed calls for a faster fossil-fuel phase-out and a $120bn adaptation target after objections from Saudi Arabia and other producers. The EU warned of a „fossil summit“; a third draft is expected today.

Agreed, not yet signed. Al Jazeera

UK and EU agree package on food checks, carbon levies and youth mobility

At the Brussels summit, PM Andy Burnham, Ursula von der Leyen and António Costa agreed fewer agri-food checks, linked emissions trading and a scheme for up to 100,000 under-30s each way. Tuition fees and „Made in Europe“ rules remain open.

German politics

Bundesrat approval. ZDFheute ticker, deutschland.de

Bundesrat clears „aktive Cyberabwehr“ and emergency-care reform

The law lets the BSI, Bundespolizei and BKA order threatening IT systems shut down, reroute traffic and read, delete or alter data during attacks; the Bundestag passed it with CDU/CSU and SPD votes. The chamber also approved the Notfallreform with new contact points and the 116 117 number, and the first part of the driving-school reform.

„Gen Z regelt“ student protests in 73 German towns

Spanish politics

Fact-check. elDiario.es

Fact-check of the Sánchez–Feijóo cara a cara

elDiario.es rated many Feijóo claims false or misleading, including that the PSOE faces criminal charges, that Spain owes €80bn in EU funds and that the Housing Ministry spent 29% of its budget (official data: 95.8%). It also faulted Sánchez's „one million homes“, which counts permits; about 752,000 were completed since 2018.

Junta Electoral opens file on PP's Tellado, clears PP AI video

The JEC says Miguel Tellado may have breached the LOREG by asking for votes on TVE before the campaign. It rejected the PSOE's complaint over a labelled AI-generated PP video.

Guardia Civil colonel convicted over rigged rifle tender

The Audiencia Provincial de Madrid gave him 18 months for presenting barracks rifles as a bidder's samples in a 2019 contract for 8,250 rifles; a contractor manager got three months.

Rail and public transport

Framework order, 18 Nov. Urban Transport Magazine

Ruhrbahn, SWK Mobil and Stoag order up to 79 Škoda low-floor trams

The NF 5 is a double-ended bogie tram in 30 m and 38 m versions, 70 km/h, for Essen, Mülheim, Krefeld and Oberhausen. In Essen it replaces the 1990s NF1 cars. Value and delivery dates were not disclosed; Škoda trams will then run in 14 German cities.

Read at Urban Transport Magazine
Declaration signed. Zughalt.de

Germany and the Netherlands make rail cooperation permanent

A joint declaration sets an annual steering committee for cross-border passenger and freight rail, with joint work on DAK, ATO and ETCS. No new services or funding were named.

Data analysis. Bahnblogstelle

Long-distance trains far more punctual in the morning

Railwise analysed 1.97 million ICE, IC and Flixtrain arrivals: 77% on time at 6 am versus 40% at 11 pm; Sundays 62%, Fridays 50%.

Fleet renewal and new long-distance coaches drive growth from €2.6bn in 2025; SCI sees saturation in flexible coaches and a cautious outlook for locomotives.
