Global headlines

Developing, day two under way. Reported by ZDFheute and Al Jazeera

Abu Dhabi talks: day one centred on a large prisoner swap

Selenskyj said the first day focused on an exchange: „Man würde gern 1.000 gegen 1.000, möglicherweise 1.500 gegen 1.500 tauschen.“ Russia raised territory, which he called „eine Frage auf der Ebene der Staatschefs“; today's agenda covers energy and Black Sea de-escalation and a possible three-president meeting. Overnight, Russia said it downed 146 drones over the Leningrad region, and NATO jets were scrambled as drones entered Latvian and Lithuanian airspace.

US House blocks Hegseth impeachment over the Iran war

The House voted 211–204 to refer Thomas Massie's resolution to committee, effectively killing it; the articles accused Hegseth of starting the Iran war without congressional authorisation. Hegseth called the effort “sabotage”; Democrats are urging him to resign.

Iran denies involvement. Reported by Al Jazeera

US charges three alleged IRGC-linked men over RAF Fairford plot

Prosecutors say the men acted for the IRGC and Kataib Hezbollah, planned to send vans disguised as fuel trucks to the UK base used by US bombers, and are linked to nearly 20 attacks or attempts in the US and Europe. Iran denies involvement in the Fairford incident.

New data. Reported by ZDFheute

Global CO2 emissions reach a new high, Global Carbon Project says

Total 2026 emissions are forecast at 43.3 bn t; fossil emissions fall 0.4%, partly as oil use dropped during the Iran war, but fires in equatorial Asia push the total up. At this rate the remaining 1.5°C budget would be used up in under four years, the COP31 delegates in Antalya were told.

German politics

Sides disagree, developing. Reported by ZDFheute

Merz and Fico clash over EU money for Ukraine

Merz wants all member states to share Ukraine's costs and calls agreement a „Vorbedingung für einen erfolgreichen Abschluss der Haushaltsverhandlungen“, asking Costa to put it on the Dublin summit agenda. Fico accuses him of „Erpressung“ („Ich werde dem nicht nachgeben“) and Babiš also refuses; a new EU loan of about €45bn needs unanimity.

Read at ZDFheute
Proposal, Union sceptical. Reported by Tagesspiegel

Klingbeil wants to abolish tax classes III and V

A Finance Ministry paper would replace them for married couples with the „automatisierte Faktorverfahren“, splitting tax by each spouse's income share. The CDU/CSU group is sceptical, adding to the tax dispute before Thursday's budget session.

Read at Tagesspiegel
Proposal. Reported by Tagesspiegel (Reuters)

SPD group pushes residence right for working refugees

A position paper would let refugees on temporary humanitarian permits stay if they work, train or study, potentially several hundred thousand, mainly Syrians and Ukrainians. Those who break rules „muss Deutschland wieder verlassen“; no Union reaction was reported.

Read at Tagesspiegel
Reported by ZDFheute

Mecklenburg-Vorpommern signs red-red-green coalition deal

SPD, Die Linke and the Greens agreed the state's first such coalition, with a „Klima-Check“ for projects and 1,000 fewer administration jobs over five years amid a gap of more than €3bn. Schwesig: „Ich will als Ministerpräsidentin fünf Jahre regieren.“ The Landtag votes on Monday.

Read at ZDFheute

Spanish politics

Developing, turnout figures from unions only. Reported by elDiario.es and Público

11-N general strike under way, the first since 2012

The housing-and-wages strike, backed by CCOO and UGT, began with „fuerte seguimiento de madrugada“ per unions: 90% in ports and „paros totales“ at Volkswagen Navarra and SEAT; no government or employer figures yet. Moncloa says it „no es contra nadie“ and Sánchez said „Esta huelga dice mucho de nuestro país y para bien“; Ayuso called the organisers „basura sindical“.

elDiario.es livePúblico
Reported by elDiario.es

PP programme adopts “prioridad nacional” for public aid

Aid would favour people with „arraigo real, duradero y verificable con España“; the PP also pledges to repeal the 2023 Housing Law, replace the Democratic Memory Law with a „Ley de Concordia“ and replace the IMV with a „renta de activación“.

Reported by Público

Government takes Valencia's “prioridad nacional” law to the Constitutional Court

The challenge targets housing and aid preferences and education provisions passed by PP and Vox; Elma Saiz said they „contradicen condiciones básicas como la igualdad entre personas“. No Generalitat reaction was reported.

Read at Público

Koldo García's lawyer resigns from his defence

Leticia de la Hoz cited „razones profesionales“ and „secreto profesional“; she had filed invoices totalling €242,000 paid to her firm by the publisher of OKdiario. The PSOE is studying a querella over García's claim that he was offered leniency to attack the government.

Rail and public transport

Reported by Zughalt.de

Rheinbahn launches tram safety programme after accident review

TÜV Rheinland, Deloitte and Human Factor Consulting found weaknesses in risk assessment, technical safety barriers and driver training after serious Düsseldorf tram accidents. An „Aktionsprogramm Betriebssicherheit“ adds competence tests, network-wide risk checks and switch safeguards; board member Christian Finke said success must be „an ihrer Sicherheitswirkung gemessen werden“.

Read at Zughalt.de
Reported by Zughalt.de

VRN wants underground platforms beneath Mannheim Hbf

DB's planned tunnel would mainly carry freight and non-stop ICEs; VRN and the city want platforms so main ICE lines keep half-hourly Mannheim stops under the Deutschlandtakt. A tunnel that only passes under the station „greife deshalb zu kurz“.

Read at Zughalt.de
Official press release, VRR

Eight transport associations launch joint Deutschlandticket campaign

The „Verbünde-Allianz“ (hvv, MVV, VBN, VVS, VRR, NAH.SH, NVV, RMV) runs shared ads in regional designs online and on billboards. VRR's Oliver Wittke: „Mit der gemeinsamen Kampagne der Verkehrsverbünde schaffen wir einen einheitlichen Auftritt.“

VDV wants ERTMS retrofit funding guaranteed to 2037

The federal guideline plans about €1.7bn through 2030 to fit ERTMS to around 12,600 vehicles; VDV says it only works if projects running past 2030 are covered by multi-year budget commitments.

Global aviation

Developing. Reported by aeroTELEGRAPH

Spain's strike could affect up to 663,000 air passengers

Minimum services require 18% to 85% of flights depending on route, with island links most protected and short mainland routes least; up to 4,600 movements may be hit, including air traffic control. Airlines and Aena had not published cancellation lists in the reports read.

Emirates freezes Berlin plans, eyes up to five 777X in late 2027

Tim Clark said Germany's cap of seven weekly flights without onward rights puts the April launch on hold: „Bis wir mehr Klarheit haben, liegt das Vorhaben auf Eis.“ He hopes for the first 777X jets towards the end of 2027.

D-AIFA “Deutschland” ferries to Munich today and starts Munich–Tokyo Haneda on 3 December, the group's first long-haul jet with Starlink WiFi. Spohr says it uses up to 30% less fuel per passenger.

Topic of the day: Open source

Official announcement, Asahi Linux. Also reported by It's FOSS

Asahi Linux moves from MIT to GPL after code was taken

The Apple Silicon Linux project is relicensing its repositories to GPL-2.0-or-later, saying unreleased code was taken without credit or rewritten with LLM help to hide its origin. BSD developers have criticised the move; OpenBSD relies on Asahi's m1n1 bootloader.
