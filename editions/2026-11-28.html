Developing. ZDFheute / Al Jazeera

US strikes ship it says tried to break Iran blockade

The US military disabled a Palau-flagged bulk carrier in the Gulf of Oman that had left Bandar Abbas and, according to the US, was trying to run its naval blockade of Iranian ports. The civilian crew was unhurt. Separately, the IRGC says a tanker was hit in the Strait of Hormuz. That claim is unverified, and Al Jazeera's blog gives no details.

Airlines. aeroTELEGRAPH / Simple Flying

Norse Atlantic's Norwegian airline files for insolvency

Lessors terminated the leases on all twelve Boeing 787-9s. Norse disputes that the terminations are valid.
