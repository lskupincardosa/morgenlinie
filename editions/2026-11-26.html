Global headlines

Trade. Al Jazeera

US proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners, including the EU

After an eight-month Section 301 probe into „structural excess capacity“, USTR proposed duties on China, the EU, Japan, South Korea, India, Switzerland, Taiwan and seven Asian economies; Mexico and Norway were spared. Rates for China and Vietnam would be 20%, and existing deals cap the EU, Japan and South Korea at 15%. China rejects the accusation, and court challenges are expected.

Saudi Arabia intercepts Houthi drones after Hodeidah strikes

The coalition says it downed drones over Ras Tanura after striking Hodeidah, Saada and Amran. The Houthis threaten Red Sea ports including Jeddah. The claims have not been independently verified.

German politics

Pensions. ZDFheute ticker

Union offers compromise on early retirement after 45 years

In a paper seen by ZDFheute, Union experts propose keeping penalty-free retirement after 45 contribution years fully intact for low earners, with deductions only for higher incomes. Separately, SPD deputy leader Wiebke Esdar urged quick income tax reform so workers see more net pay from January, and criticised Merz for now questioning the larger relief.

Rail and public transport

Infrastructure. Zughalt.de

Second track for Lübbenau–Cottbus gets under way

Work has begun on doubling about 29 km of the Görlitzer Bahn, funded from the Investitionsgesetz Kohleregionen as part of i2030. After preparatory work before the 13 December timetable change, a full closure follows, with trains diverted via Calau. The second track is due at the end of 2027 and should allow a half-hourly regional service between Berlin and Cottbus.

Global aviation

Lobbying. aeroTELEGRAPH

Ryanair quits Airlines for Europe

The co-founder leaves A4E in two weeks, Michael O'Leary said in Berlin: „Wir treten in zwei Wochen bei Airlines for Europe aus.“ The trigger is a lawsuit by ENAA, whose members include Lufthansa and Air France-KLM, against the Irish Aviation Authority over whether Ryanair is majority European-owned. O'Leary says „Unsere Struktur entspricht voll und ganz dem EU-Recht“ and is considering a new association.
