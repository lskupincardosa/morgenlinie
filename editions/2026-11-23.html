Global headlines

No decisions adopted. UN News, ZDFheute

COP31 in Antalya ends without a climate pact

After two days of overtime, the summit closed on Sunday with no agreed text: the EU, Panama and the Marshall Islands rejected a cover decision that left out fossil fuels, and COP president Murat Kurum said "there is no agreement on this text". António Guterres called the outcome "deeply disappointing" and warned that 1.5 °C will be exceeded. Environment Minister Carsten Schneider blamed oil states: „Manche Ölstaaten merken, dass die Lage ernst wird für sie - darum blockieren sie so vehement.“ Open issues move to COP32 in Addis Ababa.

German politics

Coalition debate. ZDFheute ticker

Söder wants the „Rente mit 63“ cut back; Wüst rejects his tax demand

CSU leader Markus Söder signalled room for compromise on pension reform but said „Die Rente mit 63 muss reduziert werden“; the SPD wants to keep the current rule. NRW's Hendrik Wüst (CDU) rejected Söder's call for quick compensation of cold progression, pointing to the coalition committee's choice to cut electricity tax instead.

Framework contract. Bahnblogstelle, UTM

Škoda wins up to 79 low-floor trams for Ruhrbahn, SWK Mobil and Stoag

Essen/Mülheim, Krefeld and Oberhausen jointly tendered the double-ended NF5 trams in roughly 30 m and 38 m versions, for 170 to 230 passengers at up to 70 km/h. In Essen they replace the 1990s NF1 cars, mainly on lines 103, 105, 107 and 108. Value and delivery dates were not disclosed.

Air navigation service Skeyes provides no regular control from 07:00 to 21:00, handling only emergencies and safety-critical flights. Around 429 flights with about 59,500 passengers were planned at Brussels Airport; Charleroi is also hit. Unions are protesting the government's austerity policy.
