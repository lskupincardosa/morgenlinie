Global headlines

Investigation ongoing. ZDFheute

NATO moves an AWACS east after Russian missiles land in Poland

Three Russian missiles came down in south-eastern Poland during the 19–20 November attack on Ukraine; no one was hurt, and Polish and Swedish jets did not shoot them down. Polish prosecutors say all three were new-production Ch-101s; whether they strayed or were diverted is still unclear. After a special NATO Council session on Monday, the alliance approved moving an AWACS early-warning aircraft forward on the eastern flank. Mark Rutte said Poland would be defended to „jeden Zentimeter“.

Huang back as Nvidia CEO; US blacklists Megaspeed

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang about ten days after he resigned; Trump says he lobbied the board. Nvidia shares rose about 30% and the Nasdaq 100 6.86%. Schumer wants Trump's role examined, and Commerce has put Singapore's Megaspeed and six other firms on its trade blacklist.

24 dead in Indonesian candy factory fire

Saturday's blaze gutted a three-storey factory in Cikande near Jakarta; about 30 people are still missing. A short circuit in an air-conditioner is suspected but not confirmed.

WHO's Tedros urges peace as fighting hits Ebola zone in DR Congo

Clashes near a hospital in Ituri put patients and health workers at „grave risk“, Tedros said: „The best medicine is peace.“ WHO counts 11,613 cases and 5,678 deaths as of 17 November, the country's largest Ebola outbreak.

German politics

Developing. ZDFheute

Intruder disrupts Green Party meeting in Schwerte

An unknown person stormed a Grünen meeting in Schwerte (Kreis Unna) on Monday evening, triggering a large police operation; several people were taken to hospital, though police could not yet say whether or how badly they were hurt. The suspect is being sought, and the Dortmund police Staatsschutz is investigating a possible politically motivated crime.

Spanish politics

Poll average, 23 Nov. elDiario.es

Last polls before 29N: PSOE gains, PP and Vox keep a majority

Monday was the last day polls could be published. elDiario.es's average puts the PP at 31.0% (about 128 seats), PSOE 27.8% (about 112), Vox 18.7% (about 65) and Frente Amplio 6.8% (about 11). PP and Vox together reach about 192 seats, above the 176 needed, in 9 of 10 simulations; around 48 seats are seen as still in play.

Rail and public transport

DB figures. Bahnblogstelle

Competitors reach 41.1% of German rail traffic, but the network limits growth

According to the Wettbewerbsbericht 2025/2026 published by DB, 524 companies used the German network in 2025 and competitors' share rose 1.4 points to 41.1%; passenger rail grew 3.4%. DB InfraGO renewed 2,092 switches and commissioned 64 signal boxes, yet „ein überlastetes und sanierungsbedürftiges Netz begrenzt das Wachstum“.

Global aviation

Grounding unconfirmed. aeroTELEGRAPH

Plus Ultra halts ticket sales, possibly ending operations

The Spanish long-haul airline no longer sells tickets, even on its last Caracas routes, after filing for creditor protection last week. Spanish media report the final flights may have been on 22–23 November; this is not confirmed. Fuel costs are expected to reach about €105m in 2026, it still owes €53m on its COVID-era state loan, and investigations into that aid are ongoing.

Topic of the day: Self-hosting

Release model. OpenCloud blog

OpenCloud puts Production releases behind a subscription from April 2027

The free, public Rolling channel continues with a release roughly every three weeks, while patched, twice-yearly Production releases will require a Basic, Professional or Premium subscription. The licence is unchanged and the code stays public; OpenCloud cites the cost of long-term maintenance and says this is not a move to open core.
