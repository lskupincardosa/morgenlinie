Global headlines

Developing. Al Jazeera

Hormuz traffic down over 40% as the Gulf war spreads to Yemen and Riyadh

Iran's navy says oil tankers hit mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz on Tuesday; Al Jazeera's reports differ on whether two or three ships were hit, and there is no independent confirmation. Crossings fell from 226 (1–7 Nov) to 125 (17–23 Nov), per Marine Traffic data. The Saudi-led coalition has ordered civilians out of Yemen's Hodeidah port, the Houthis threaten Jeddah and other Saudi ports if it is attacked, and the coalition says Riyadh's PIF tower was targeted in a „terrorist attack“; who was behind it is unconfirmed.

Spanish politics

Last cabinet before 29N. elDiario.es

Birth leave rises to 20 weeks; 10 days for bereavement

In its final meeting before Sunday's election, the Consejo de Ministros used a real decreto-ley to extend paid, non-transferable birth and care leave from 17 to 20 weeks per parent, and to 34 weeks for single-parent families. It also approved 10 days' leave on the death of a family member. Sánchez: „Cumplimos con el compromiso de alcanzar las 20 semanas en el permiso de nacimiento y cuidado.“

Topic of the day: Open source

Release. PostgreSQL

PostgreSQL 19 released

The 19 November release adds REPACK (CONCURRENTLY) to reclaim bloat without blocking reads or writes, plan-stability tools (pg_plan_advice), parallel index vacuuming in autovacuum and replication of sequence values. JIT is now off by default, and md5 passwords trigger warnings.

Release announcementLWN
Licensing. LLVM forum

LLVM ends its eight-year relicensing effort

Over 99.5% of all code ever contributed is now under Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception or gone: „We think that's a good moment to call the effort complete.“ Licence files will be updated for LLVM 25.
